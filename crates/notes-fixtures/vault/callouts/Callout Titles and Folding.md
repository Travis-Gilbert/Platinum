# Callout Titles and Folding

> [!tip] A custom title
> The body.

> [!tip]
> No title, so the type's name is the title.

> [!faq]- Folded by default
> Hidden until opened.

> [!faq]+ Open by default, and foldable
> Visible until closed.

> [!note]-
> Folded, with no title text.

> [!info] Title with **bold**, `code`, a [[Headings Target]] link and a #callout-tag
> Body with a list:
>
> - one
> - two
>
> And a code block:
>
> ```js
> const x = 1;
> ```
>
> And an embed:
>
> ![[pixel.png]]

> [!note] Title only

> [!danger] Two paragraphs
> The first paragraph.
>
> The second paragraph.

> [!example] Table and math
> | a | b |
> | - | - |
> | 1 | 2 |
>
> $x^2$
